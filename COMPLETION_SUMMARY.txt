================================================================================
                      SWITCHING PROVIDERS
================================================================================

TO SWITCH TO CASHFREE (Current Setup)
  1. Update "Provider": "Cashfree"
  2. Add KeyId and KeySecret
  3. Set CashfreeEnvironment: "sandbox" or "production"
  4. Configure webhook: /api/v1/payments/webhooks/cashfree
  5. Restart application

TO SWITCH TO RAZORPAY (If Needed)
  1. Update "Provider": "Razorpay"
  2. Add KeyId and KeySecret
  3. Add WebhookSecret
  4. Configure webhook: /api/v1/payments/webhooks/razorpay
  5. Restart application
